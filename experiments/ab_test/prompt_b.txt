1. cinematography — 逐镜时间轴 shot_timeline（每个镜头：index, start_sec, end_sec, shot_type, camera_movement, visual_content, transition_to_next）+ 宏观统计 macro（total_shots, avg_shot_length_sec, dominant_camera_height_range, dominant_lighting, visual_emotion_curve）
